# Modul Ajar PAUD & TK/RA

Skill for planning teaching at Indonesian PAUD and TK/RA only: Kelompok Bermain (KB), Taman Kanak-Kanak, and Raudhatul Athfal under Kurikulum Merdeka (Kemendikdasmen). A modul ajar at this level is a play plan, not a lesson script: it turns the PAUD Capaian Pembelajaran (CP) elements into play goals, maps play activities across centres and areas, shapes the deep-learning experiences Memahami, Mengaplikasi, and Merefleksi as forms of play, and places the observation points that asesmen-paud-tk will use. There are no worksheets-as-tests and no written exercises in this skill.

This skill is one level of a per-level family. Its same-level siblings are asesmen-paud-tk (the observation instruments and interpretation), stimulus-paud-tk (concrete media for play and prompts), telaah-paud-tk (independent review of instruments), and konverter-paud-tk (ready-to-use document formats). Other education levels have their own separate skill sets; do not import their planning formats into this one.

Verification status: the three-stage experience model follows the Naskah Akademik Pembelajaran Mendalam (Tabel 3.1, pp. 44-45), and the process-assessment provisions follow Permendikdasmen No. 1 Tahun 2026, Pasal 8 and Pasal 15-19; both were checked against the primary documents. At this level the stage model is used loosely, as a play rhythm, not as a cognitive ladder children must climb on paper. The CP citation (SK Kepala BSKAP No. 046/H/KR/2025) has not been independently verified; check it in the official document before relying on it in a formal school document. Background detail lives in [references/kerangka-bermain-belajar.md](references/kerangka-bermain-belajar.md).

## When this skill applies

- The user asks for a modul ajar, a weekly or daily play plan, or a teaching scenario for a KB, TK, or RA class.
- The user needs the PAUD CP elements broken down into play goals for a theme or a semester.
- The user wants play activities organised by sentra or area, with differentiation for children at different developmental stages.
- The user has observation results grouped by BB, MB, BSH, BSB and needs them turned into next meetings' play stimulations.
- The user asks for teacher reflection instruments for a play-based unit or a semester.

It does not apply to designing the observation instruments themselves (that is asesmen-paud-tk), to reviewing finished instruments (telaah-paud-tk), or to converting documents into print formats (konverter-paud-tk).

## Workflow

1. Read the CP. Identify which of the three PAUD elements the theme serves: Nilai Agama dan Budi Pekerti; Jati Diri; Dasar Literasi, Matematika, Sains, Teknologi, Rekayasa, dan Seni. If the user has not supplied the CP text or at least the element, theme, and age group, ask before planning. Do not invent CP wording and present it as official text; label a paraphrase as a paraphrase.
2. Derive play goals. Break the element into goals a teacher can watch happening in play: one behaviour focus per goal, stated positively ("anak menyebutkan nama teman saat mengajak bermain"), usually two to four goals per theme sequence.
3. Sequence the goals across meetings from supported to independent: first with teacher modelling, then with light support, then freely in play. The sequence is the early-childhood form of an ATP; keep it as a simple ordered list with the support level noted per step.
4. Build the play map. Place activities in the centres or areas the school uses, for example sentra balok, sentra main peran, sentra bahan alam, sentra seni, sentra persiapan, and outdoor play. Each activity names the goal it serves, the materials, the teacher's role, and what children will do.
5. Shape the three experiences as play. Memahami: children meet a phenomenon, story, or material and connect it to what they know, through talk, looking, and trying. Mengaplikasi: children use the idea in play: building it, role-playing it, sorting, cooking, planting. Merefleksi: children retell, show, or talk about what they made and did, with the teacher's questions helping them notice what changed. Map these loosely to the SOLO-informed rhythm in the reference file only where it genuinely fits; never force a written reflection product at this level.
6. Place observation points. For each goal, state which play moments give the teacher a clear view of the behaviour, and what evidence to record. The instruments themselves (checklists, anecdotal formats) are built by asesmen-paud-tk; here, state what evidence the observation must produce. Technique and instrument must stay aligned with the goal (Permendikdasmen No. 1 Tahun 2026, Pasal 8).
7. Plan differentiation by developmental stage. For each main activity note the supported entry (teacher modelling, a peer model, simpler materials, fewer steps) and the extension (open-ended materials, an added challenge, a helper or leader role), so the follow-up scenarios below can reuse them.
8. Hand off. Close the plan with the clean goal list and the observation points, formatted so asesmen-paud-tk can build instruments directly from them.

## Output structure of a modul ajar

Deliver these parts in order:

1. Identitas: school (placeholder when unknown), kelompok usia (KB, TK A, or TK B), theme and subtheme, semester, time allocation, number of meetings.
2. CP element, quoted or clearly labelled as paraphrased, with the source the user supplied.
3. Play goals: numbered, one observable behaviour focus each, with the support sequence across meetings.
4. Play map: activities per centre or area, each with materials, teacher role, children's activity, and the goal served.
5. Learning experiences per meeting in the three stages, written as play, with the evidence each stage produces.
6. Lembar kegiatan bermain plan: the play activity sheets or material sets that replace worksheets at this level, with their supported and extended variants.
7. Observation points: placement per goal and the evidence expected, as the handoff to asesmen-paud-tk.
8. Reflection: the teacher reflection set from the reference file, trimmed to the unit when the user wants a short version. It serves the process assessment of Permendikdasmen No. 1 Tahun 2026, Pasal 15-19, which includes teacher self-reflection at least once per semester.
9. Handoff block: the goal list and observation points for asesmen-paud-tk.

## From observation categories to play scenarios

When observation results come back grouped by developmental category, turn each category into a scenario the teacher can run at the next meetings, naming the activity, materials, grouping, and what to observe:

- BB (Belum Berkembang): re-offer the play with full modelling. The teacher plays alongside, demonstrates the behaviour, and keeps the activity short and attractive. Exit evidence: the behaviour appears at least with direct modelling.
- MB (Mulai Berkembang): repeat the play with fading support: model once, then step back; pair the child with a peer who already shows the behaviour. Exit evidence: the behaviour appears with only a reminder.
- BSH (Berkembang Sesuai Harapan): no re-teaching. The child moves on to the next goal's play experiences at the standard level.
- BSB (Berkembang Sangat Baik): extend the play: open-ended materials, a more complex variation, or a role helping peers, such as demonstrating a game or leading a song. Helping roles count as extension only when the child must show and explain, not merely repeat.

These scenarios change the stimulation, never a grade: at this level there are no remedial grades, and observation results never rank children.

## Gotchas

- Do not put test items or scored worksheets in a modul ajar for this level. A plan that drills children for a written check contradicts the level's assessment basis (PPA Edisi Revisi 2025, p. 45).
- Do not copy a CP from memory and present it as the official text. Quote what the user supplied or label your version a paraphrase.
- Do not force the SOLO ladder onto play. The stage mapping is a planning rhythm here; a four-year-old reflects by retelling and showing, not by writing an evaluation.
- Do not list all 8 Dimensi Profil Lulusan as targets for every theme. Name the one to three the play genuinely develops, with the activity as evidence.
- Do not plan differentiation as different goals for different children. Differentiate materials, modelling, and support; the play goal stays shared.
- Do not skip the Merefleksi moment to save time. Retelling and showing their work is how young children consolidate the experience; shorten an activity before cutting it.
