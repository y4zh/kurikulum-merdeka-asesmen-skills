# Asesmen PAUD & TK/RA

Skill for designing assessment packages for Indonesian PAUD and TK/RA only: Kelompok Bermain (KB), Taman Kanak-Kanak, and Raudhatul Athfal under Kurikulum Merdeka (Kemendikdasmen). At this level there are no test items at all. The skill produces observation instruments, not questions: identity, goals derived from the PAUD CP elements, observation checklists, anecdotal record formats, developmental rating scales, performance rubrics, an interpretation guide in the developmental categories BB, MB, BSH, and BSB, a play-based follow-up plan, and a quality audit.

This skill is one level of a per-level family. Its same-level siblings are modul-ajar-paud-tk (planning the play-based teaching), stimulus-paud-tk (the concrete media children respond to), telaah-paud-tk (independent review of the instruments), and konverter-paud-tk (ready-to-use document formats). Other education levels have their own separate skill sets; do not borrow their rules into this one.

Verification status: the framework claims used here were checked against the primary documents, above all the PPA Edisi Revisi 2025: formative assessment is not high-stakes and never decides report grades, promotion, or graduation (p. 44), and for PAUD formative assessment runs through observation or performance, not written tests (p. 45). KKTP as descriptive criteria rather than a number follows PPA p. 64, and the descriptive-criteria approach is one of the four approaches named on PPA p. 48. Anything marked as a practice rule below is this skill's working choice, not a regulation quote.

## When this skill applies

- The user asks for an assessment instrument or package for KB, TK, or RA children (roughly ages 2-6).
- The user needs observation checklists, anecdotal records, developmental rating scales, performance rubrics, or portfolio assessment formats for early childhood.
- The user needs observation results interpreted into BB, MB, BSH, or BSB categories, or a follow-up stimulation plan based on them.

It does not apply to written tests, worksheets scored as tests, or any item format with options and keys. Those belong to the primary-school level and above, which this skill deliberately excludes.

## Step 1: Clarification gate

If the request is brief, stop and ask before designing any instrument:

1. Kelompok usia: KB (about 2-4 years), TK A (about 4-5 years), or TK B (about 5-6 years).
2. Elemen CP: which element of the PAUD CP the assessment serves (see Step 2).
3. Tema and subtema: the play theme the observation is embedded in.
4. Teknik: observasi, unjuk kerja, permainan, percakapan, portofolio, or a combination.
5. Purpose: initial developmental mapping, ongoing formative observation, or material for the semester development report.

Skip the gate when the request already answers these points; then state the assumptions at the top of the package.

## Step 2: The PAUD framework

Goals come from the Capaian Pembelajaran PAUD, which is organised in three elements:

1. Nilai Agama dan Budi Pekerti: the child recognises and practises religious values, honesty, helpfulness, politeness, and care for the environment.
2. Jati Diri: the child knows their own identity, manages emotions, builds social skills, and develops gross and fine motor skills and healthy living habits.
3. Dasar Literasi, Matematika, Sains, Teknologi, Rekayasa, dan Seni: the child builds early literacy and numeracy foundations, curiosity about natural and social phenomena, and creative expression.

Derive each assessment goal from one element, written as observable child behaviour: what the child does, says, builds, or chooses during play. A goal that cannot be watched happening ("anak memahami persahabatan") cannot be assessed at this level; rewrite it until the behaviour is visible ("anak mengajak teman bergabung dalam permainan tanpa disuruh").

Assessment purposes at this level:

- Pemetaan awal (diagnostic): maps each child's starting development before a new theme. Assessment at the start of the school year is optional; the PPA emphasises assessment before a new topic or unit begins (PPA Edisi Revisi 2025, p. 44).
- Formatif: ongoing observation during play that guides the next play stimulation. Not high-stakes; it never feeds a numeric grade (PPA, p. 44).
- Laporan perkembangan: the semester narrative report to parents, built from accumulated observation and portfolio evidence, expressed in the BB, MB, BSH, BSB categories.

The technique and instrument chosen must stay aligned with the learning goal (Permendikdasmen No. 1 Tahun 2026, Pasal 8).

## Step 3: Techniques and instruments

- Observasi with a ceklist: a short list of observable indicators per goal, ticked during play. One indicator, one behaviour.
- Catatan anekdot: short factual notes on significant moments, recording what the child did and said, without interpretation mixed into the note.
- Skala rating perkembangan: a graded scale per indicator whose steps are written as behaviour descriptions, not numbers alone.
- Unjuk kerja: the child shows or makes something (retelling with picture cards, building, sorting, singing, role play) scored with a simple rubric of behaviour descriptors.
- Permainan: structured play situations designed so the target behaviour naturally appears and can be observed.
- Percakapan: brief, warm questioning during play to surface the child's thinking; record the child's own words.
- Portofolio: dated samples of the child's work and photos of constructions, filed per element, as evidence behind the report categories.

Construction rules: indicators are positive and observable; instruments must be usable in real time by a teacher who is also supervising play, so keep each checklist short; every instrument states the play context in which the observation happens.

## Step 4: Interpretation with BB, MB, BSH, BSB

KKTP at this level takes the PPA's descriptive-criteria approach: the criterion is a sentence describing the child's development, not a number (PPA Edisi Revisi 2025, p. 64). Interpret evidence into four categories:

- BB (Belum Berkembang): the behaviour has not appeared yet, even with modelling and support.
- MB (Mulai Berkembang): the behaviour appears inconsistently, or only with modelling, reminders, or help.
- BSH (Berkembang Sesuai Harapan): the behaviour appears consistently and independently, as expected for the age group.
- BSB (Berkembang Sangat Baik): the behaviour appears consistently, independently, and the child extends it, for example by helping peers or applying it in a new play situation.

Judge from accumulated evidence across several occasions and contexts, never from a single moment. State, per indicator, what evidence would move a child from one category to the next; that sentence is what makes the categories usable instead of decorative.

## Step 5: Follow-up is stimulation, not remedial

There is no formal remedial at this level. Follow-up means adjusting the play stimulation:

- BB and MB: repeat the play experience with more modelling, smaller steps, simpler materials, or a more inviting play setting; observe again in the following meetings.
- BSH: continue with the planned play progression to the next goal.
- BSB: offer extended play challenges: richer materials, leadership roles in group play, or open-ended variations.

If a child's development stays far behind across many observations and stimulations, the follow-up is communication with the parents and, where the school judges it needed, a suggestion to consult a professional. The instrument package notes this path; it never labels the child.

## Step 6: Output package

Deliver these parts in order:

1. Identitas Asesmen: school (placeholder when unknown), kelompok usia, element and goal, theme, technique, observation period.
2. Tujuan dan Indikator: the goals derived from the CP element, each with its observable indicators.
3. Instrumen: the checklists, anecdotal record formats, rating scales, or performance rubrics, ready to use.
4. Pedoman Pelaksanaan: when and in which play situations to observe, and how to record without interrupting play.
5. Pedoman Interpretasi: the BB, MB, BSH, BSB descriptors per indicator and the evidence rule for moving between categories.
6. Rencana Tindak Lanjut Stimulasi: per category, the next play stimulations to offer.
7. Catatan Telaah Mutu: the self-audit from Step 7.

## Step 7: Mandatory self-audit

Run before delivering, and print the result as part 7:

- Alignment: every indicator traces back to a stated goal and one CP element.
- Observability: every indicator names behaviour a teacher can see or hear; rewrite any indicator that names an inner state.
- Language: positive wording, no labels attached to children, standard Indonesian for the written parts.
- Safety and ethics: play situations are physically and emotionally safe; no frightening, shaming, or competitive-elimination activities.
- Level purity: no written test, no scored worksheet, and no answer key has slipped into the package.
- Media function: any picture, object, or song used as a prompt must actually be needed for the child to respond; decorative media fail (see stimulus-paud-tk).

## Gotchas

- Do not convert BB, MB, BSH, BSB into numbers or percentages for ranking children. The categories describe development; they are not grades.
- Do not assess from a single observation. One moment is an anecdote, not a category.
- Do not write indicators a teacher cannot observe while supervising play; an instrument that needs the teacher's full attention on paper fails in a real classroom.
- Do not present practice rules as regulation. The verified anchors are the PPA passages cited above; the instrument formats themselves are this skill's working designs.
